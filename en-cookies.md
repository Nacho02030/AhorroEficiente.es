## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device to remember information about your visit. Equivalent technologies (device identifiers, local storage) are covered by this same policy.

## 2. What this site uses

- **Strictly necessary cookies:** this site does not need to set its own cookies in order to work.
- **Audience measurement:** Cloudflare Web Analytics is used, which according to its provider does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.
- **Advertising:** the site may display Google AdSense advertising. In that case, Google and its partners may use cookies and identifiers to serve ads, and will do so only with your consent where the law requires it.
- **Third-party content:** images hosted on Pixabay load from its servers, which may receive your IP address.

## 3. How to manage your preferences

Once the site carries advertising, a notice will let you accept, reject or configure non-essential cookies, and you can change your decision at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; note that this may affect how some sites work.
