## How content is produced

Guides are drafted with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools from our own topic planning, and pass automated quality checks before publication (minimum length and rejection of defective text). The editor oversees the site periodically and corrects any errors reported.

## Principles

- **Prudent rigour:** we avoid presenting as fact what cannot be verified; figures are given as orders of magnitude or ranges.
- **Independence:** no company funds or influences the guides. If the site carries advertising, it will be clearly distinguishable from editorial content and will not influence it.
- **Transparency:** the calculation tools show their assumptions.

## Corrections

Corrections are made as soon as an error is found. To report one, write to nachojimenezcor@gmail.com.
